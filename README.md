# Transcriptomic Investigation of EZH2-Associated Pathways in G7 Glioblastoma Stem Cells
## Overview
This repository contains the computational transcriptomic analysis performed as part of my MSc thesis project investigating EZH2-associated molecular pathways in G7 glioblastoma stem cells. 
The project focuses on identifying differential gene expression patterns and biologically relevant pathways associated with epigenetic dysregulation in glioblastoma.
## Background
Glioblastoma (GBM) is an aggressive primary brain tumour characterized by high cellular heterogeneity and therapeutic resistance.
EZH2, a catalytic subunit of the Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2), plays a critical role in chromatin remodeling and transcriptional repression in cancer. The dysregulation of EZH2 has been implicated in glioblastoma stemness, proliferation, and tumour progression. This project investigates transcriptomic alterations associated with EZH2-related signaling in G7 glioblastoma stem cells.
## Objectives
- Investigate transcriptomic alterations associated with EZH2 dysregulation in G7 glioblastoma stem cells.
- Identify differentially expressed genes (DEGs).
- Perform pathway and gene ontology enrichment analysis.
- Explore epigenetic and proliferation-associated molecular mechanisms in glioblastoma.
